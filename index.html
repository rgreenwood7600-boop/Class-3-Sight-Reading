For the examiner
  1. Choose the instrument, then press Generate Sight Reading, or pick a passage by number.
  2. Record the passage number before the candidate plays. The same number is printed on the PDF.
  3. The music is written pitch for that instrument, in the key and time marked, at the tempo on the score.
  4. A new draw will not repeat the passage just shown.
